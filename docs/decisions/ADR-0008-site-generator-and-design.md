# ADR-0008: Website generator (Python + jinja2) and design system

- **Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Status:** accepted

## Decision
The website is generated by `tools/build_site.py` (jinja2 templates in `tools/site/`) instead of Quarto. That gives one toolchain (Python), which already reads the YAML. The same data also feeds the indicator preview, the Verification Desk and the Claude skill.

## Design
- Art direction was dealt by the tasteskill anti-slop process: direction "the page is the object", seed 1382301316, sector education, tone editorial.
- Adopted idea: Miʿyar (العيار = the assay standard of gold). **Official text is a gold sheet with green ink and a hallmark**; commentary is gold ink on the bottle-green ground; AI-assisted text sits on a raised green panel and always carries its label.
- Typeface: Amiri for Arabic and Latin. This is a stated redraw, because the dealt face, Boska, has no Arabic.
- House rules: no gradients, no rules except in tables, no shadows at rest, square corners, five type steps.
- Live element: the ruler on the home page, comparing availability, search period and age across 10 countries, using numbers from verified quotes only.

## Consequences
- Deployment: `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, manual only. The lead enables Pages once (Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions) and runs the workflow.
- Private preview: `tools/prepare_site_preview.py` → artifact.
- Open-collection images could not be reached from the sandbox. The pictures are the lead's PCBS screenshot and photographs of the project's own tools; real photographs (NSO buildings, conference archives) are a backlog item (I-048).
