# ADR-0005: Licensing (proposed, pending the lead's confirmation)

- **Date:** 2026-11-03 · **Status:** proposed

## Proposal
- Original content written by the project (explanations, comparisons, glossary entries): **CC BY 4.0**. This allows reuse with attribution, which spreads the brand and gets the work cited.
- Code (tools, site, skills): **MIT**.
- Quoted official texts remain under their publishers' terms. They are quoted briefly with attribution and links (see `prd/12-legal-privacy.md`).

## Alternatives considered
CC BY-NC 4.0 would protect the paid training material. Recommendation: keep the core open under CC BY and keep **training decks, exercises and datasets** under a separate, non-open licence.
