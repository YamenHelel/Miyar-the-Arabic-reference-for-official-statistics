# 09: Business model: a free core with paid services around it

## Free (always)
Website, all indicator and concept pages, source registry, microdata catalogue, the public Claude skill, the directory.
**Why:** being free is what creates reach, citations and trust, and those are what sell the paid services.

## Paid
| Offer | Target | Format | Indicative price (to test) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI for official statistics** training | NSO staff, regional bodies | 2–5 days, online or on-site, Arabic | per-day consultant rate or per-participant fee |
| **19th ICLS / informality (21st ICLS) implementation** workshop | LFS teams | 3 days with labs on real questionnaires | same |
| **Statistical writing and communication with AI** | Communication units | 1–2 days | same |
| Methodology and questionnaire review | NSOs, projects | Consulting days | daily rate |
| Custom AI assistant for an NSO (their metadata + this base, private) | NSOs | Project | fixed price + maintenance |
| Ready-made training packs (slides, exercises, datasets) | Trainers, universities | Licence | per-pack licence |
| Sponsored domain build | Regional and international bodies | Project funding | per domain |

## Rules to protect credibility
- Paid work never changes the free content. Sponsors are acknowledged but have no editorial control.
- Consulting clients' confidential material never enters the public base.

## Validation steps
1. Run the demand survey (I-036) together with the directory launch.
2. Pilot one paid training in Phase 1 with a friendly institution.
3. Collect testimonials and publish case studies, with the client's permission.
