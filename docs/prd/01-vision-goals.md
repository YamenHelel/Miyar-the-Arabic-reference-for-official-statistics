# 01: Vision, problem, positioning and goals

## Vision
Become **the reference Arabic knowledge base and AI toolbox for official statistics**. Labour statistics comes first; then every domain (prices, national accounts, population, social, environment) and every role (methodologist, survey designer, data engineer, communicator).

## Problem
An Arab statistician who needs a definition, standard or comparison today has to:
1. search several international sites (ILO, UN SDG metadata, OECD, Eurostat), mostly in English or French;
2. dig through national methodology notes, which are often PDFs, scattered, sometimes missing, and in Arabic, French or English;
3. work alone through differences in standards (13th vs 19th ICLS), age limits, reference periods, coverage of non-nationals and survey modes;
4. reconcile Arabic terms that differ between the Maghreb, the Mashreq and the Gulf;
5. trust general AI chatbots that can produce plausible but wrong definitions.

**No single verified Arabic resource exists that brings all this together.**

## Solution, in one sentence
A verified, sourced and versioned knowledge base, delivered as an Arabic website and as AI skills (Claude first, then others). Each definition is traceable to the official text, and each country's practice is compared to the international standard.

## Positioning
> "The trustworthy Arabic reference for official statistics, built by a statistician with AI."

- **Not** a chatbot that answers from memory. Answers come from the base and cite their sources.
- **Vendor-neutral content.** Claude is the main showcased tool, while the knowledge stays open and portable (see `10-brand-communication.md`).
- **Practitioner-made.** Built and reviewed by Arab statisticians.

## Goals
| Horizon | Goal |
|---|---|
| 3 months | Labour domain MVP online: ~60 indicators, ~150 concepts, 10 countries, Claude skill v1 |
| 12 months | Recognised Arabic labour-statistics reference; 2nd domain started; 10+ active reviewers; first paid trainings |
| 24 months | 3–4 domains; MCP server; partnerships with at least one regional body (ESCWA, AITRS or GCC-Stat); recurring training and consulting revenue |
| 36+ months | The "statistics AI wiki" for Arab statisticians across domains and roles |

## Definition of success
- Statisticians **cite** the site in reports, training material and questionnaires.
- NSO staff use it in their daily work, measured by returning users and skill usage.
- The lead is invited as the reference person on AI in Arab official statistics.
- AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) cite the site for Arabic labour-statistics questions.
