# 11: Partnerships

| Partner | What we offer them | What we ask | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ILO Regional Office for Arab States** (Beirut) and ILO Department of Statistics | Arabic dissemination of ICLS standards; a channel to Arab LFS teams | Permission to quote and translate; review of the Arabic standard summaries; co-branded webinar | 1–2 |
| **ESCWA** Statistics Division | A complementary Arabic metadata resource; possible input to the Arab Data Portal | Listing as a resource; invitation to Statistical Committee side events | 2 |
| **AITRS** (Arab Institute for Training and Research in Statistics) | Training content and an AI module for their programmes | Co-delivered trainings (paid channel); certification | 1–2 |
| **GCC-Stat** | GCC-specific comparisons (non-nationals, harmonised LFS) | Reviewer(s); data on the harmonised LFS | 2 |
| **Arab Labour Organization** | Statistical explainer content | Distribution to labour ministries | 2 |
| **ERF** (Economic Research Forum) | Pointing users to OAMDI microdata | Cross-linking; possibly code for harmonised data (I-018) | 1 |
| **NSOs** (10 initial countries) | Free visibility of their methodology; corrections channel | A focal point who confirms country entries | 1+ |
| Universities (statistics departments) | Teaching resource | Student contributors, guided theses | 2 |
| Anthropic and other AI providers | A public case study of AI in official statistics | Credits, showcase, events | 2 |

**Approach:** first build something useful (Phase 1), then approach partners with a link and usage numbers. Do not ask for endorsement before there is something to show.
