# 02: Users and personas

## Primary personas (Phase 1: labour)
| Persona | Context | Typical questions | What they need |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSO labour statistician** (LFS team) | Produces quarterly LFS results | "How does the 19th ICLS define employment? What changes if we move from ICLS-13?" | Exact standard text, migration notes, examples from peer countries |
| **Survey designer** | Designs or revises the LFS questionnaire | "Which questions does the ILO model questionnaire use for job search?" | Model questionnaires, question wording in Arabic, country practices |
| **Methodologist / sampler** | Sampling, weighting, variance | "How do I compute the standard error of the unemployment rate?" | Concept glossary with formulas, worked examples, code |
| **Data analyst / economist** (ministry, central bank) | Uses published figures | "Why is Jordan's rate different in ILOSTAT and DoS?" | Comparability notes, source catalogue |
| **SDG focal point** | Reports SDG 8 indicators | "What is the metadata for 8.5.2 and who is the custodian?" | SDG metadata summaries in Arabic, with links |
| **Communicator / journalist** | Writes press releases and articles | "What is the correct Arabic term for 'labour underutilization'?" | Plain-Arabic explanations, terminology |
| **Student / trainee** | Learns official statistics | "What is the difference between inactive and unemployed?" | Simple explanations, quizzes |
| **Trainer / consultant** | Builds training material | "I need a slide on LU1–LU4 with Arab country examples" | Reusable, citable content (paid tier: ready-made decks) |

## Secondary (later phases)
Regional and international organisations (ESCWA, GCC-Stat, ILO ROAS), researchers using microdata, policy makers, and data engineers building dissemination systems.

## Constraints of our users
- Many work behind institutional firewalls and have no paid AI subscription → **the website must work without AI**.
- Mixed language background: Maghreb (Arabic and French), Mashreq and Gulf (Arabic and English).
- Mobile use is common → pages must be phone-friendly.
- Low trust in AI for official work → every answer must show its source.
