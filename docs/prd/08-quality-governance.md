# 08: Quality and governance

## Content lifecycle
`draft` (AI or contributor) → `reviewed` (a second person checked it against the source) → `verified` (lead or editorial board sign-off, dated). Pages show their status and last-verified date.

## Verification checklist (for each entry)
1. Does the source open, and is it the official publisher?
2. Is it the latest edition? If not, set `superseded_by`.
3. Does the quoted text match the source word for word?
4. Is the Arabic rendering faithful? Is it marked official or unofficial?
5. Are country details (age, reference period, standard) confirmed by a document, not inferred?
6. Are the archive link and access date recorded?

## AI-use policy (published on the site)
- AI (Claude) is used to collect candidate sources, draft explanations, translate and structure.
- AI output is never published as an official definition.
- Every AI-assisted text is labelled «شرح مُعدّ بمساعدة الذكاء الاصطناعي ومُراجع بشرياً» (explanation prepared with AI assistance and reviewed by a human).
- The skill answers only from the verified base and cites its sources.

## Editorial board (from Phase 1)
- One reviewer per country (ideally a current or former NSO staff member), plus a methodologist and a terminology reviewer.
- Reviewers are credited on the pages they review.
- Conflicts of interest are declared (e.g. a reviewer from the same NSO whose figures are being discussed).

## Corrections
A public "suggest a correction" path on each page. Target: triage within 7 days. Corrections are logged in the CHANGELOG.

## Neutrality
The project describes and compares; it does not rank or criticise NSOs. Comparability notes are factual and sourced.
