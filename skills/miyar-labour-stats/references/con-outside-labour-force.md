# خارج القوة العاملة — Outside the labour force / Hors de la population active (inactifs)

Miʿyar id: `con-outside-labour-force` · category: labour

## Official definition

**Source:** Resolution concerning statistics of work, employment and labour underutilization (19th ICLS, 2013) — PDF, as amended by the 21st ICLS (2023) — ILO  
**URL:** https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/@dgreports/@stat/documents/normativeinstrument/wcms_230304.pdf  
**Locator:** 19th ICLS resolution, paragraph defining the labour force (§16 approx.)  
**Status:** VERIFIED official text (by YH, 2026-11-04)  
**Note:** Text as published in the 19th ICLS resolution amended by Resolution II of the 21st ICLS (Geneva, 11–20 October 2023).  
**Language:** en

> 16.Priority is given to employment over the other two categories, and to unemployment over
> outside the labour force. The three categories of labour force status are, thus, mutually
> exclusive and exhaustive. The sum of persons in employment and in unemployment equals the
> labour force. Persons outside the labour force are those of working age who were neither in
> employment nor in unemployment in the short reference period.
